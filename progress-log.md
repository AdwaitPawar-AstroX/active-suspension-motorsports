# 📈 Progress Log

A chronological record of development milestones, challenges encountered, and decisions made throughout the project.

---

## March 2026

### ✅ Milestones
- Completed full system concept and architecture definition
- Finalised suspension type — F1-style double wishbone pushrod selected over MacPherson and multi-link alternatives
- Completed mechanical design and all component specifications
- CAD modelling completed for all major assemblies
- Physical components acquired for prototype build
- Selected PETG-CF as prototyping material for all structural components
- Project formally approved with LIDAR predictive mechanism as core innovation

### 🔄 In Progress
- Evaluating actuator configuration — parallel vs replacement approach under R&D
- MATLAB/Simulink quarter-car simulation model being developed
- PID controller initial implementation underway

### 🧩 Challenges Faced
- **Actuator placement** — determining optimal position of micro linear actuator relative to mechanical damper and rocker geometry. Two configurations under evaluation. Further simulation needed before committing.
- **Rocker geometry** — balancing mechanical advantage ratio with packaging constraints inside chassis

### 💡 Key Learnings
- Pushrod-rocker architecture provides significantly better packaging for inboard actuator placement compared to direct-acting designs
- PETG-CF provides sufficient stiffness for prototype validation while allowing rapid design iteration without machining cost
